TubeReady

Auto-detect YouTube videos and get them ready to download.

yt-dlp helper

Checking…

Chrome cannot run yt-dlp itself. Double-click install-host.bat in the TubeReady folder once, then reload the extension. Videos save to Downloads\TubeReady.

How to use

  1. Open any YouTube video or Short.
  2. TubeReady detects the link automatically.
  3. Click the red button on the page, or the extension icon.
  4. Pick a quality and download.

Downloads use yt-dlp after you run install-host.bat. If the helper is missing, TubeReady falls back to in-page streams or a Cobalt instance.

Only save videos you have the right to download — your own uploads, Creative Commons, or other permitted content. YouTube’s terms still apply.
