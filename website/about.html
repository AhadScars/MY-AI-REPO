About DigiHub Solutions

A software company for every platform

We design and build Android apps, iOS apps, web applications, websites, and custom software — with optional ready-made billing products for stores that need them.

Our story

DigiHub Solutions began by helping local businesses replace messy spreadsheets with practical software. That taught us something lasting: good tools should be clear, reliable, and learnable without an IT department.

We grew from focused business apps into a full software studio. Today we build Android APK apps, iOS apps, web applications, websites, APIs, and custom desktop tools for startups, brands, and growing companies in any industry.

Alongside custom work, we still offer ready-made Pharmacy POS, Restaurant POS, Hotel PMS, and School Management for owners who need billing software quickly. Custom projects and POS products share the same DigiHub standard: clean UX, solid engineering, and human support.

We work directly with founders and teams who want clear scope, fair quotes, and launch help — from first idea to production. No forced marketplace lock-in. No feature maze.
