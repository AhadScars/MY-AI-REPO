I built an AI tool that turns any video into clear notes you can read and listen to.

🎥 How it works
1. Upload a video (processed in your browser)
2. AI analyzes key frames with Google Gemini
3. Get notes: summary, timeline, key points, takeaways
4. Choose language: English · हिन्दी · اردو
5. Listen with Play / Pause / Speed
6. Pick Female or Male voice
7. Hover a paragraph to hear just that part
8. Chat follow-ups: quizzes, action items, one-line takeaways

Why I built it: learning from videos is faster when notes + audio + language choice work together.

🔗 Open source on GitHub:
https://github.com/AhadScars/Ai-Video-Analyzer
